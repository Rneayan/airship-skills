# Triage policy

## Hard gates

Review these first when they have a current date or consequence:

1. Client delivery or requested reply
2. Tax, legal, corporate filing, payment, or evidence deadline
3. Academic submission, registration, or teaching obligation
4. Scheduled medical follow-up or required document
5. A dependency blocking another committed deliverable

High stakes requires authoritative verification; it does not authorize submission or payment.

## Scoring aid

Use scoring only to compare already-confirmed actions.

- Urgency: 0 none, 1 this month, 2 this week, 3 within 72 hours, 4 overdue with current consequence
- Consequence: 0 optional, 1 useful, 2 committed/reputational, 3 legal/financial/health/client delivery
- Readiness: 0 blocked, 1 unclear next action, 2 executable now
- Energy cost: 0 under 15 minutes, 1 focused block, 2 multi-block or emotionally heavy

Prefer high urgency + consequence + readiness. Reduce scope when energy cost is high.

## Output buckets

- Today: exactly one primary action, with a concrete completion condition
- This week: up to two secondary actions and fixed-date obligations
- Waiting or verify: blocked items with the missing input and follow-up date
- Decision needed: stale items requiring do, defer, delegate, or drop
- Reference checklist: excluded from active workload

Translate these labels to the user's language while preserving their meanings.

## De-duplication

- Use a canonical Project or Area note for background.
- Use the most recent dated task for timing.
- Treat a weekly priority that links to a project checkbox as one obligation, not two.
- Preserve conflicting dates and flag them for resolution.
